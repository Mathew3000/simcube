panel orientation check

A known asymmetric pattern is written straight into the panel buffers instead of running the simulation. Water settling symmetrically cannot reveal a mirrored face; this can.

On every face, viewed from outside, you must see: a red square at the bottom-left (texel 0,0), a green bar running right along +x, and a blue bar running up along +y.

Red anywhere but bottom-left means that face is flipped or mirrored. Right-drag to orbit.
